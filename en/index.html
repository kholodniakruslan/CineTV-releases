CineTV is a free app for movies and TV shows

CineTV puts a catalog of movies, series, cartoons and anime in one app. Posters, ratings, descriptions, cast and trailers come from TMDB in your language, while the app looks the video up in connected sources and plays it in a built-in player.

It runs on Android TV, Windows and macOS and works the same everywhere: a home screen with new releases, search, a movie page with dubbing and quality choice, Continue watching, favorites and history. There are no ads, no sign-up, and your data stays on your device.

How to watch movies on Android TV, Windows and macOS

Movies on Android TV

Download the APK for your processor (arm64-v8a for most boxes and TVs) and install it on Android TV 10 or newer. The app works with a regular remote: arrows, OK and Back.

Installation guide

Movies on Windows

Run the installer or unpack the portable version. You need Windows 10 or newer, 64-bit. .NET and the video player are already inside the app, so there is nothing else to install.

Installation guide

Movies on macOS

Open the .dmg for your Mac: Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) or Intel. macOS 12 Monterey or newer is required. The builds are not signed yet, so on first launch you confirm opening them as the guide describes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads, no sign-up and no account.

Where does the video come from?

The catalog, posters and descriptions come from TMDB. The app does not host any video: it looks the title up on third-party source sites and shows what it finds. You are responsible for the content you watch.

Is it safe?

The app never asks for an account, passwords or payment details. Settings, history and favorites stay on your device. Before installing an update the file checksum is verified, and on Android the package name and signature too.

Why do Windows and macOS warn me on launch?

The builds are not signed with developer certificates yet, so SmartScreen and Gatekeeper show a warning. The installation guides explain how to open the app.

What quality is available?

It depends on the source: HDRezka and sites on the HDVB player give up to 1080p, Filmix without its own account and the Collaps player up to 720p. Maximum quality is set per source.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is in English, Russian and Ukrainian, defaulting to your system language. The dubbing language is chosen on the movie page.

How do I update the app?

It offers a new version on launch. Updates are offered for stable versions only.

Can I suggest a new source?

Yes. Open a source suggestion on GitHub and give the site address, the dubbing language and what makes it good.

Ready to watch?

Installing takes a couple of minutes. No sign-up needed.
